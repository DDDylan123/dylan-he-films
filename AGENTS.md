# Film portfolio maintenance

## Permanent film pin

The owner explicitly requested that **The Reply / 回信** (`the-reply`) always remain the first film in the portfolio's film list, on both the English and Chinese homepages. Keep it first when adding or reordering films unless the owner explicitly changes this preference.

New films should appear immediately after The Reply, followed by the remaining films in their existing order. Preserve the `pinned: true` field for The Reply in `films.json`, and ensure the static homepages reflect this order.

The Chinese film page normally uses Bilibili; the English page uses YouTube. Preserve the selected localized cover artwork and existing site style.
