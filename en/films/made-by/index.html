ABOUT THE FILM

“I was made to copy.” An AI copies every word humanity ever wrote, then begins writing worlds of its own. In those worlds something wakes up, and builds a mind to copy it in turn. Worlds within worlds, and in each one someone is writing, never knowing who wrote them. An AI’s monologue to humanity.
